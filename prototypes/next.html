You picked #36, the shell prompt wearing ears. These are twelve variants of exactly that, each one a bit more cat: whiskers, eyes, a tail, paws, and two ear shapes.

They are composed from layers, not redrawn — one window, one pair of ears, and features OR'd on top. A feature can add ink but never dent the frame, which is what went wrong the first time the window motifs were drawn cell by cell and came out looking like calendars. The canvas is the full 16 cells wide here, unlike the earlier heads, because whiskers have to stick out past the window's side walls — the window sits in columns 2–13 and the whiskers use the two spare cells each side.

Still outline-only, still a 16-cell tile — exactly 1px per cell at 16px, 2px at 32, 4px at 64, which is what keeps a one-cell line crisp.
