Claufy icons — round three, all line art

Everything from before is gone. These are outlines: a one-cell black edge, the tile showing straight through the middle, and the eye and mouth in the same black. Nothing is filled. Half the set is a side view — sitting, walking, loafing, asleep — because twelve versions of one front-facing head is what made the last round samey.

They all sit on a 16-cell tile, which is why the thin lines survive: 16 cells is exactly 1px per cell at 16px, 2px at 32, 4px at 64. The icon that shipped used an 18-cell tile — 0.888px per cell — so no edge landed on a pixel and every "pixel" came out a different width. That is what looked uneven in the toolbar, and a one-cell outline would show it far worse than a solid shape did.
